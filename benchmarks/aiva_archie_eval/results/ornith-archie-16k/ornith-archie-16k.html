How Grace Works

The AIVA PC companion runtime. A request leaves you, travels through the Console WebUI and rosbridge, is adapted by telepathy, served by the llama.cpp router to Ministral, and returns — backed by SQLite memory and vector Canvas sidecars, orchestrated by launch.sh.
